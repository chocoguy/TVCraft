# Roadmap

## 1.0: Minecraft PE 0.8.1 on Apple TV
- Playable on Apple TV hardware with a controller, with sound and saves.
- TVCraft Installer: build and install from your own APK, updates from GitHub releases.

## Long term: Minecraft PE 1.1.5
A functionally equivalent decompilation of 1.1.5 is under way in [MCPE-1.1.5-decomp](https://github.com/chocoguy/MCPE-1.1.5-decomp). Once it builds and plays, TVCraft moves to it.
